About Us

Founded on Hustle.
Built on Passion.

Q & M Lawncare was founded in 2022 by Quinlan Marsh — the eldest brother — while he was still a student at Watauga High School. His goal was simple: try something new and make it work. With the help of friends and family, what started as a side hustle has grown into a thriving lawn care business.

We offer everything from basic lawn mowing and trimming to creating landscaping walkways and full property transformations. Quinlan and his crew bring great work at an affordable price — fair pricing, no surprises. Reach out today for your free consultation and quote.

That dedication is exactly why we were voted a 2025 Nextdoor Neighborhood Fave — an honor voted on by the real neighbors and community members we serve every day. Quinlan's dream is to make Q & M Lawncare a lasting career, and every yard is a step toward that goal.
